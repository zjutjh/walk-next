# ZHEJIANG UNIVERSITY OF TECHNOLOGY JINGHONG UNION "The 16th JINGHONG ENDURANCE WALK" EVENT GUIDELINES AND DISCLAIMER

## PART I — EVENT GUIDELINES

### I. Registration Guidelines

#### (1) Self-Assessment

The Jinghong Endurance Walk is a medium- to high-intensity, long-distance outdoor hiking event initiated and co-organized by the Jinghong Union of Zhejiang University of Technology. Participation requires an appropriate level of physical fitness. Individuals with any of the following conditions should not participate:

1. Congenital heart disease or rheumatic heart disease;
2. Hypertension or cardiovascular or cerebrovascular disease;
3. Myocarditis or another heart condition;
4. Coronary artery disease or a serious cardiac arrhythmia;
5. Diabetes accompanied by excessively high or low blood glucose;
6. Any other condition for which exercise is medically inadvisable.

Participants must carefully assess their actual condition, truthfully consider their health status, and take responsibility for their own safety. Anyone who conceals a health condition and participates shall bear the resulting consequences. This clause does not affect any safety, warning, emergency-response, or other duty that Jinghong Union is required to perform under applicable law or agreement.

#### (2) Registration Requirements

1. **Eligibility.** Applicants must have full capacity for civil conduct, understand their own physical fitness and condition, and be able to objectively assess the difficulty and risks of the Endurance Walk. A minor who does not have full capacity for civil conduct may participate only with the consent of the minor's legal guardian and must be registered by that guardian. The applicant, and the legal guardian registering on the applicant's behalf, must carefully read these Guidelines and sign this Disclaimer.

2. **Registration Method.** Registration is team-based. Participants must provide identity and contact information, including full name, Resident ID number, school ID number, and mobile phone number of the participant or emergency contact. (Note: Sensitive information such as ID card numbers shall be used solely for identity verification and purchasing event-related insurance during the duration of this event; personal or emergency contact numbers shall be used exclusively for personal or emergency communication during the event period and will not be integrated into the trekking system.) When creating or subsequently forming a team, carefully verify the selected route and team composition.

3. **Registration Confirmation.** After registering, participants are advised to follow official event WeChat accounts, including "ZJUT Jingxiaohong Online" (浙工大精小弘在线) to receive notices promptly, and to check relevant text messages.

### II. Guidelines During the Event

#### (1) Before Departure

1. The departure time and assembly point for each campus are subject to the official text-message notice.
2. The team leader must ensure that all expected team members are present before the team queues for departure.
3. Participants must check in sequence at the starting point and collect a checkpoint card. Keep the card safe; completion results will be determined by the card.

#### (2) Checkpoint Rules

1. A team may check in only when at least half of its members are present. Each checkpoint must stamp the card in sequence; checkpoints may not be skipped.
2. Some checkpoints have cut-off times. Plan your pace accordingly. After a checkpoint closes, participants must not continue forward and must leave the route via the designated withdrawal point nearest that checkpoint.

#### (3) Precautions En Route

1. The event offers multiple long-distance routes. Select a route suited to your condition. If you experience physical discomfort, exhaustion, or another emergency, promptly contact event staff for assistance in withdrawing and ending your participation. Do not overexert yourself.
2. The Endurance Walk requires teamwork. Support and cooperate with your teammates and other teams, and avoid becoming separated.
3. Take measures appropriate to your condition to prevent heatstroke, insect bites, and sun exposure. Walk carefully and proceed in an orderly manner on steep slopes. Do not remain at one location for an extended period if doing so may cause congestion. Avoid conflicts with others; contact event staff for assistance when necessary.

### III. Safety Guidelines

1. The Organizer will work with professional support personnel and volunteers to provide crowd and order management, route guidance, and emergency-response services throughout the event. The Endurance Walk is not scored as a timed race. Participants need only complete the designated distance within the prescribed time and should select a pace appropriate to their team's actual condition.

2. In the event of sudden illness, injury, exhaustion, or another emergency, immediately contact the nearest staff member or volunteer wearing an identification badge and wait in place for medical assistance. Do not move unnecessarily, as doing so may cause further injury. Staff will assist in contacting medical services and activating the emergency-response plan.

3. Smoking and open flames are prohibited. Autumn conditions may be dry; take particular care to prevent wildfires.

4. The event may involve risks, including without limitation, traffic accidents, falls, falls from cliffs, drowning, high temperatures, and attacks or injuries caused by animals or other organisms, any of which may result in injury or death. Jinghong Union has provided the necessary risk warnings and will take reasonable safety measures. Participants must independently assess these risks and voluntarily assume the consequences associated with participation. This clause does not affect any other protective duty that Jinghong Union is required to perform by law.

5. In the event of severe weather, a geological disaster, or another event of force majeure, the event may be postponed, rerouted, or cancelled. Follow official notices closely and comply with on-site instructions.

### III. Liability Waiver, Release of Claims, and Compensation Provisions

1. **Nature of the Event and Voluntary Assumption of Risk.** This is a non-commercial, non-profit, self-supported outdoor activity open only to Zhejiang University of Technology students, faculty, staff, and alumni. It follows the principles of self-help, mutual assistance, and environmental protection. The event organizers and participants have equal participant status. Article 1176 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, concerning voluntary participation in activities carrying inherent risk, applies throughout the event.

2. **Scope of the Event Organizers' Duties.** The non-profit Event Organizers undertake coordination, organization, and safety-warning duties within reasonable limits. In the event of force majeure, a sudden safety incident, or another major matter, participants shall cooperate in addressing the situation. Participants must comply with overall event arrangements, observe travel and event rules, and safeguard their own safety.

3. **Risk Disclosure.** The Jinghong Endurance Walk is affected by weather, terrain, and other factors and involves inherent risks that may be unforeseeable. I have fully assessed my physical condition and understand that I may encounter severe weather, complex terrain, sudden illness, falls, falling rocks, and other accidents, potentially resulting in serious bodily injury or death. The event may also take place far from professional rescue services, such that effective assistance may not be available promptly. The Event Organizers will take safety measures within the limits of their capabilities.

4. **Rescue and Medical Expenses.** If any such injury occurs, I agree to bear rescue, medical, and related expenses, or any amount remaining after insurance payment, except to the extent that those expenses arise from the intentional misconduct or gross negligence of the Event Organizers, team leaders, or fellow participants.

5. **Transportation.** I understand that self-driving, chartered transport, or other mutually arranged travel to or from the event also involves unforeseeable traffic risks. I voluntarily choose whether to ride in or drive a relevant vehicle. Article 1217 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, concerning non-commercial carriage offered as a favor, shall apply where legally applicable. If a traffic accident causes injury or death, claims shall first be submitted under the insurance covering the vehicle involved. For loss beyond available insurance coverage, I and my family voluntarily waive claims for additional compensation against the driver or vehicle owner, the Event Organizers, and fellow participants, except where the loss is caused by the driver's intentional misconduct or gross negligence.

6. **Insurance Claims.** I understand that the Event Organizers are not an insurer but have arranged relevant accident insurance for participants. I confirm that the Event Organizers have informed me of the insurance arrangements and that I understand the coverage and exclusions. I undertake that the health and identity information I provide for insurance purposes is truthful. Any accident claim shall first be submitted to the underwriting insurer in accordance with the policy. Except where loss is caused by the intentional misconduct or gross negligence of the Event Organizers or fellow participants, I agree to accept compensation within the policy coverage and will not seek additional compensation from the Event Organizers. I shall bear consequences or losses resulting from denial or reduction of coverage caused by my failure to disclose my circumstances truthfully.

7. **Health Commitment and Personal Safety Duties.** I represent that I am in good health and fit to participate and do not have a condition that may affect event safety or the safe use of equipment, including, without limitation, heart disease, hypertension, asthma, or another condition capable of sudden onset. I will reduce risk by monitoring the environment and my physical and mental condition, using clothing and equipment appropriate for safety, and participating only within my capabilities. I understand that personal alertness and judgment are essential to my safety and will stop participating immediately if I consider the risk to exceed my capacity. Except where personal injury or property loss is caused by the intentional misconduct or gross negligence of the Event Organizers, team leaders, or fellow participants, I voluntarily bear the relevant consequences.

8. **Mutual Assistance and Limits of Rescue.** If unforeseeable circumstances, including an accident, sudden weather change, or acute illness, cause physical harm, the Event Organizers and fellow participants shall make reasonable efforts to assist and provide aid within reasonable limits. If irreversible harm remains despite reasonable rescue efforts, I will not seek compensation or joint and several liability from those persons, except where the harm is caused by their intentional misconduct or gross negligence. When another participant needs assistance, I will cooperate with the initiator and other participants to provide help only to the extent that I reasonably can without exposing myself to unreasonable danger. I understand that the Event Organizers and fellow participants have reasonable mutual-assistance duties but do not provide an absolute safety guarantee or assume unlimited residual liability.

9. **Proxy Registration and Effect of this Declaration.** Before registration, I have communicated fully with my emergency contact or family regarding the nature and potential risks of the event and this Declaration, and have obtained their understanding. I confirm that I have carefully read and voluntarily accepted all terms. This Declaration applies to me. If an accident results in my loss of capacity for civil conduct or death, its provisions concerning voluntary assumption of risk and the exclusion of organizer liability where there is no intentional misconduct or gross negligence shall also apply to my statutory heirs, close relatives, executors, administrators, personal representatives, and assigns. If another person registers and signs on my behalf, that proxy must disclose all terms truthfully and completely and obtain my express consent. The proxy shall bear legal consequences arising from a failure to perform that disclosure duty that renders this Declaration ineffective against me.
